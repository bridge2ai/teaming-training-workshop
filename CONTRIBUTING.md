# Contributing to Teaming Training Workshop

Thank you for your interest in contributing to this project!

By submitting a contribution (e.g., a pull request, issue, or any other addition or modification to the repository), **you agree that your contribution will be licensed under the same CC BY 4.0 license** that covers this project. Please ensure that any content you contribute is compatible with this license and that you have the right to submit it under these terms.

## How to Contribute

1. **Fork the repository** and create a new branch for your changes.
2. **Make your changes** — whether fixing a typo, improving content, or adding new materials.
3. **Submit a pull request** with a clear description of the changes you have made and why.

## Content Guidelines

- Ensure all contributed content is original or properly attributed and compatible with CC BY 4.0.
- Do not include material that is under a more restrictive license (e.g., content that prohibits commercial use or derivatives) without explicit permission.
- Provide attribution for any third-party content you include, as required by its original license.

## Code of Conduct

Please be respectful and constructive in all interactions. We aim to maintain an inclusive and welcoming environment for all contributors.

## Questions

If you have any questions about contributing, please open an issue or reach out to the project maintainers.
